FAQ

Questions pastors ask

Does the Front Door Audit cost anything?add

No. The audit is free, and you don't need a call to get your scorecard. If you want to go over it together, you can book a free 30-minute call with our team afterward.

Can you tell who showed up?add

Everyone who plans a visit lands on your page and gets a text and an email from us. On Sunday your welcome team marks who arrived with a simple check-in link. That's how the report shows real guests and your campaign budget per guest.

Isn't marketing a church a little strange?add

We think of it as an invitation. Most people come because someone invited them, and that hasn't changed. Our job is to put your pastor's own invitation in front of neighbors and make the first visit easy. Your members' invitations still matter most, and the videos give them something worth sharing.

Do you promise a number of guests?add

No. Your count depends on your town and your campaign budget. It also needs your welcome team to mark each arrival. The guest report shows the real number each month, so your leadership can judge the campaign for themselves.

Do we have to commit to a term?add

Yes, 6 months or 12. A campaign needs a few months to bring families through your doors, and a term lets us plan your year with you. Your term starts the day your campaign launches. After it ends, you continue month to month at the same rate and can cancel with 30 days notice.

Do we own the videos?add

Yes. Everything we build for your church stays yours: the videos, your plan-your-visit page, the follow-up messages and your guest list. Post the videos anywhere you like.

Is the campaign budget separate from your fee?add

Yes. It's what Meta charges to show your invitation to families near your church. The church pays it from its own Meta account, straight to Meta, and we never mark it up. Budgets start at $500 a month.

Do you film and edit yourselves? Do you use AI?add

Chris films at your church and edits your videos himself. AI tools help with transcripts and rough cuts, and nothing reaches you until he has watched it. Nothing that speaks to what your church believes goes out without your approval.

We're not in Texas. Can we still work together?add

Yes. Travel inside Texas is included. Outside Texas, your church covers travel for filming days, at cost plus a $350 travel day. Campaign management works from anywhere.
