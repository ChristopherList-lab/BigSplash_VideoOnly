The follow-up plan

We follow up with every family

A family who visits once should hear from you again. We text them from a number in your church's name. After they come back twice, we hand them to your team.

  1. Saturday

    We send a reminder text: "We saved you a seat." Your welcome team gets a list of who's coming.

  2. Sunday afternoon

    We text a thank-you and 4 quick questions about their first Sunday. You can offer a small gift to families who answer.

  3. The next six Sundays

    Each Sunday afternoon, we send a short text to see who came back.

  4. Didn't reply

    A month later, we text them another invite. We also show them an online invite that only they see.

  5. Came once

    Two months later, someone on your team checks in with them. They also see that invite.

  6. Came back twice

    We hand them to your team for a next step, like kids ministry or a small group. Our marketing stops there.

  7. Christmas and Easter

    We send a holiday invite to every family who hasn't replied STOP.

FAQ

Questions pastors ask

Is the Front Door Audit free?add

Yes, it's free, and you don't need a call. Want to go over your scorecard together? Book a free 30-minute call with our team.

Can you tell which families came?add

Yes. Families who plan a visit get a text and an email from your church. Your welcome team gets the list on Saturday. On Sunday, they use a simple link to check off each family they greet. Your report counts each family and shows your campaign budget per family.

Do the follow-up texts come from our church?add

Yes. We set up a texting number in your church's name. Families can reply STOP anytime. We write the texts, and you approve each one before it goes out.

Should a church advertise?add

We see it as an invitation. Most people visit a church because someone invited them. We put your pastor's invitation in front of your neighbors. Then we help you welcome the families who come. Your members' invitations still matter most, and the videos give them something to share.

Can you promise how many families will come?add

No. The number depends on your town and your campaign budget. We can only count the families your welcome team checks off on Sunday. Each month, your family report shows the real number. Then your leaders can decide if it's working.

Is there a long-term contract?add

No. You sign a simple month-to-month agreement and can cancel with 30 days notice. Most campaigns take a few months to bring families through your doors. Give it a season before you judge it.

Do we own the videos?add

Yes. Everything we build for your church stays yours. That includes the videos, your plan-your-visit page, the follow-up messages and your list of families. Post the videos anywhere you like.

Do we pay for the ads on top of your fee?add

Yes. Your church pays Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to show your videos, straight from its own account. We call this your campaign budget. It starts at $500 a month, and we never mark it up.

Do you edit the videos yourselves? Do you use AI?add

Chris edits every video himself. If you add a filming day, he films at your church too. AI helps with transcripts and rough edits, and Chris watches everything before you see it. You approve anything about your church's beliefs before it goes out.

We're not in Texas. Can we still work together?add

Yes. We can run your campaign from anywhere. If you add filming, travel inside Texas is included. Outside Texas, your church pays travel costs plus a $350 travel-day fee.
