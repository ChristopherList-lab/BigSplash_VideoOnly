FAQ

Questions pastors ask

How do you know who actually showed up?add

Everyone who plans a visit lands on your page and gets a text and an email from us. On Sunday your welcome team marks who arrived with a simple check-in link, or through Planning Center Check-Ins if you already use it. That's how the report shows real guests and what each one cost.

Isn't marketing a church a little strange?add

We think of it as an invitation. Most people come because someone invited them, and that hasn't changed. Our job is to put your pastor's own words in front of neighbors and make the first visit easy. Your members' invitations still matter most, and the clips give them something worth sharing.

Who edits our pastor's message? Do you use AI?add

AI builds the rough cut and pulls the transcript. Our editing team chooses the moments and polishes every clip, and a Big Splash lead in Texas approves each one before it reaches you. Nothing that speaks to what your church believes goes out without your approval.

What is the campaign budget?add

It's what Meta charges to show your invitation to families near your church. The church pays it from its own ad account, straight to Meta. It's separate from our fee and we never mark it up.

Do you post the clips for us?add

Snapshot clips are yours to post. On Reach we send 4 clips a week and your team posts them. From Growth up we post and manage your Facebook, Instagram and YouTube for you.

What is the Google Ad Grant?add

Google gives qualifying nonprofits, including many churches, up to $10,000 a month in free search ads. Your church applies through Google for Nonprofits, and on Growth and Partner we set up the account and keep it healthy so it stays active.

Why does filming on a Sunday cost more?add

A Sunday covers every service, plus worship, the lobby and baptisms, with a highlight video the same week. It's the longest day for our crew and the day every church wants. Moving a Partner filming day to a Sunday adds $600. A single Sunday for other churches is $1,200. Christmas and Easter book 60 days out.

We're not in Texas. Can we still work together?add

Yes. Reach and Growth are fully remote and work anywhere. Partner works anywhere the church covers travel, at cost plus a $350 travel day.
