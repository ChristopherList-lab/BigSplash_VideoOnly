Follow-up, step by step

No family falls through the cracks

A family who visits once deserves a second invitation. We follow up with every new family from your church's own number, and once they come back, we hand them to your team.

  1. Saturday

    A reminder text: "We saved you a seat." Your welcome team gets the list of families coming.

  2. Sunday afternoon

    A thank-you text and a 4-question survey about their first Sunday. You can add a small thank-you gift for answering.

  3. The next six Sundays

    A short check-in text each Sunday afternoon, so we know who came back.

  4. No answer

    A re-invite a month later, plus an invitation ad shown only to them.

  5. Came once

    A personal follow-up from your team two months later, plus the invitation ad.

  6. Came back twice

    We hand them to your next steps, like kids, groups or serving, and the marketing stops.

  7. Christmas and Easter

    Every family who hasn't opted out gets the invitation.

FAQ

Questions pastors ask

Does the Front Door Audit cost anything?add

No. The audit is free, and you don't need a call to get your scorecard. If you want to go over it together, you can book a free 30-minute call with our team afterward.

Can you tell which families came?add

Yes. Each family who plans a visit lands on your page and gets a text and an email from your church. On Saturday your welcome team gets the list of families coming, and on Sunday they mark each family they welcomed with a simple check-in link. That's how the report counts real families and your budget per family welcomed.

Do the follow-up texts come from Big Splash?add

No. We set up a texting number in your church's name, so every text comes from your church, and families can reply STOP anytime. We write the messages and load them, and you approve each one before launch.

Isn't marketing a church a little strange?add

We think of it as an invitation. Most people come because someone invited them, and that hasn't changed. Our job is to put your pastor's own invitation in front of neighbors and help your church welcome the families who come. Your members' invitations still matter most, and the videos give them something worth sharing.

Do you promise a number of families?add

No. Your count depends on your town and your campaign budget. It also needs your welcome team to mark each family. The family report shows the real number each month, so your leadership can judge the campaign for themselves.

Is there a contract?add

No. The partnership runs month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days notice. A campaign usually needs a few months to bring families through your doors, so give it a season before you judge it.

Do we own the videos?add

Yes. Everything we build for your church stays yours: the videos, your plan-your-visit page, the follow-up messages and your list of families. Post the videos anywhere you like.

Is the campaign budget separate from your fee?add

Yes. It's what Meta charges to show your invitation to families near your church. The church pays it from its own Meta account, straight to Meta, and we never mark it up. Budgets start at $500 a month.

Do you film and edit yourselves? Do you use AI?add

Chris films at your church and edits your videos himself. AI tools help with transcripts and rough cuts, and nothing reaches you until he has watched it. Nothing that speaks to what your church believes goes out without your approval.

We're not in Texas. Can we still work together?add

Yes. Travel inside Texas is included. Outside Texas, your church covers travel for filming days, at cost plus a $350 travel day. Campaign management works from anywhere.
