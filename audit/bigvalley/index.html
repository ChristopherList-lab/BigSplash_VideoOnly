02 · How This Audit Works

THE RUBRIC.

Every line item is scored on a five-point scale. The score reflects how a first-time visitor would experience the brand at that touchpoint, not whether the asset technically exists. A logo that loads is a 1. A logo that loads, looks crisp, sits in correct clear-space, and matches the rest of the system is a 5.

5
Dialed In
Matches spec. On-brand across every angle. Nothing to fix.
4
Strong
Reads on-brand at a glance. Small details could tighten.
3
Drifting
Recognizable, with visible inconsistencies. Worth fixing this year.
2
Off-Brand
Wrong color, old logo, stale artwork. First impression takes a hit.
N/A
Not Scored
Touchpoint does not apply, or no asset exists yet to evaluate.

Three pillars roll up to the overall score. Online and In Person each weigh forty points. Governance weighs twenty.

ONLINE

Every digital surface a visitor lands on before Sunday. Website, social profiles, email, listings, and digital asset templates.

IN PERSON

Every physical touchpoint from the parking lot to the lobby. Signage, print, stage, events, apparel, and guest-facing materials.

GOVERNANCE

The behind-the-scenes system that keeps the first two pillars from drifting. Brand guide, asset library, vendors, and review cadence.

06 · The Foundation

GOVERNANCE IS THE GAP.

Five governance items are blank. Not low. Blank. No brand guide, no shared asset library, no vendor list with print specs, no named audit owner, no review cadence. Every fix on the next pages stays fixed only if these five are in place.

Brand Guide

The Single Source
  • Logo, color, type, voice in one PDF—
  • Linked from staff onboarding doc—
  • Updated when assets change—

Asset Library

One Place, Always Current
  • Logos, fonts, colors, templates—
  • Old logos removed from drives—
  • Permissions set for staff—

Vendor List

No More Print Surprises
  • Approved print + signage vendors—
  • Pantone, paper stock, coatings—
  • Apparel decoration specs—

Owner + Cadence

Who, How Often
  • Named brand owner on staff—
  • Quarterly mini-audit—
  • Annual full audit—

A brand guide is a one-time build. The asset library is a half-day setup. The vendor list is a Google Doc. The owner is a name. Without these four pieces, your 80 will slip back to 72 inside a year as new staff make new files.

08 · The Bar

WHAT A 5 LOOKS LIKE.

A 5 is not a perfect church. A 5 is a touchpoint that does not make a guest pause. Here is the standard for the eight items that drag the score down today.

✓

Favicon

The brand mark, on a transparent background, exported at 16, 32, 48, and 192 px. Loads crisp in the browser tab. Matches the social profile photo at a glance.

✓

Brand Colors

Hex, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone codes locked in the brand guide. Every button, header, and chart on the site uses the same six values. No off-by-one greens.

✓

Social Cover Art

Refreshed each sermon series. Same series art that lives on the stage screen, cropped for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and the website hero. One artwork, four crops.

✓

Bulletin Template

One INDD or Canva file. Locked layout, type styles, and color. Volunteers fill copy, not formatting. Every Sunday's bulletin reads like the same church made it.

✓

Livestream Backgrounds

Lower thirds, scene transitions, and pre-stream loop all share palette and type with the stage. The livestream feels like attending, not watching from a different church.

✓

Exterior Sign

Current logo, current color, lit at night, readable from the road at 35 mph. Matches the lobby sign a guest sees thirty seconds later. The drive in and the walk in connect.

✓

404 Page

Same header and footer as the site. A short on-brand line. A button back to the homepage. Not a default server page that breaks the spell.

✓

Visitor Packet

Designed like the sermon series, not like an HR onboarding doc. One folder, three inserts, a connect card, and a small gift. Hands a first-timer a reason to come back.

10 · Process + Timeline

THE 90-DAY PATH.

Twelve weeks from kickoff to a 95. Each phase is short, scoped, and tied to a deliverable a guest will see on a Sunday.

Week 01 · Kickoff

LOCK THE FOUNDATION.

Ninety-minute kickoff with the brand owner and senior staff. Pull every existing logo, font, and template into one shared drive. Confirm scope, roles, and review cadence.

Deliverable: Asset library, named owner, signed scope
Weeks 02 – 03 · Brand System

THE GUIDE.

Refine the logo lockups, lock color and type tokens, and ship the brand guide PDF. Approved vendor list and print specs live inside the same document.

Deliverable: Brand guide v1, vendor + print specs
Weeks 04 – 06 · Digital + Documents

SHIP THE TEMPLATES.

Favicon, 404 page, bulletin template, livestream backgrounds, document headers, and social cover artwork. Every digital fix on page 8 lands in this window.

Deliverable: 10 templates, social pack, branded 404
Weeks 07 – 09 · Physical Touchpoints

FIX THE PERIMETER.

Exterior signage rework, wall art update, and a redesigned visitor packet. Coordinate with the approved print vendor from the guide. Install around a Sunday with low foot traffic.

Deliverable: Exterior sign, wall graphics, visitor packet
Weeks 10 – 11 · Polish + Train

HAND IT OFF.

Train the brand owner and the comms team on the guide and the asset library. Run a tabletop walkthrough of the next bulletin, slide deck, and social post using the new system.

Deliverable: Staff training, walkthrough recording
Week 12 · Re-Audit

SCORE THE RESULT.

Repeat this audit end-to-end. Confirm the score moved from 80 to 90 or higher. Set the next quarterly mini-audit on the calendar before the engagement closes.

Deliverable: Re-audit report, quarterly cadence on calendar
