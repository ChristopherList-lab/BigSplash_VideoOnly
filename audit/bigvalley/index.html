02 · How This Audit Works

THE RUBRIC.

Every line item is scored on a five-point scale. The score reflects how a first-time visitor would experience the brand at that touchpoint, not whether the asset technically exists. A logo that loads is a 1. A logo that loads, looks crisp, sits in correct clear-space, and matches the rest of the system is a 5.

5
Dialed In
Matches spec. On-brand across every angle. Nothing to fix.
4
Strong
Reads on-brand at a glance. Small details could tighten.
3
Drifting
Recognizable, with visible inconsistencies. Worth fixing this year.
2
Off-Brand
Wrong color, old logo, stale artwork. First impression takes a hit.
N/A
Not Scored
Touchpoint does not apply, or no asset exists yet to evaluate.

Three pillars roll up to the overall score. Online and In Person each weigh forty points. Governance weighs twenty.

ONLINE

Every digital surface a visitor lands on before Sunday. Website, social profiles, email, listings, and digital asset templates.

IN PERSON

Every physical touchpoint from the parking lot to the lobby. Signage, print, stage, events, apparel, and guest-facing materials.

GOVERNANCE

The behind-the-scenes system that keeps the first two pillars from drifting. Brand guide, asset library, vendors, and review cadence.

08 · The Bar

WHAT A 5 LOOKS LIKE.

A 5 is not a perfect church. A 5 is a touchpoint that does not make a guest pause. Here is the standard for the eight items that drag the score down today.

✓

Favicon

The brand mark, on a transparent background, exported at 16, 32, 48, and 192 px. Loads crisp in the browser tab. Matches the social profile photo at a glance.

✓

Brand Colors

Hex, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone codes locked in the brand guide. Every button, header, and chart on the site uses the same six values. No off-by-one greens.

✓

Social Cover Art

Refreshed each sermon series. Same series art that lives on the stage screen, cropped for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and the website hero. One artwork, four crops.

✓

Bulletin Template

One INDD or Canva file. Locked layout, type styles, and color. Volunteers fill copy, not formatting. Every Sunday's bulletin reads like the same church made it.

✓

Livestream Backgrounds

Lower thirds, scene transitions, and pre-stream loop all share palette and type with the stage. The livestream feels like attending, not watching from a different church.

✓

Exterior Sign

Current logo, current color, lit at night, readable from the road at 35 mph. Matches the lobby sign a guest sees thirty seconds later. The drive in and the walk in connect.

✓

404 Page

Same header and footer as the site. A short on-brand line. A button back to the homepage. Not a default server page that breaks the spell.

✓

Visitor Packet

Designed like the sermon series, not like an HR onboarding doc. One folder, three inserts, a connect card, and a small gift. Hands a first-timer a reason to come back.
