Hey babe. Welcome back to Big Girl Thoughts. For episode 03, I sat down with Janessa Waite. You probably know her shop — the posters, the trucker hats, the "He still does miracles" line. What you may not know is that the whole thing started with a Canadian girl waiting on a green card, a friend's iPhone, and a still small voice that said "you could do this too." She picked up a ballpoint pen. Eleven years later, there's a seven-figure company, a warehouse, seven employees, and a book she wrote for postpartum mamas called A Heart So Full. If you have ever felt the nudge and let fear talk you out of it, this one is for you. Janessa came on to tell you not to bow to it. WHAT WE GET INTO 0:00 Hi, hi, hi. ABOUT JANESSA Janessa Waite is the founder of shop.janessawait.com, the Christian home-goods and apparel company behind the "He still does miracles" hat and a whole lot of posters that end up in nurseries. Canadian by birth, Austin by call, wife to Sean, mama to two with one on the way, and a former young-adult pastor who still pastors anyone who buys a hat.
