04 · How to run it

Test & Compare, in four steps.

YouTube Studio has a built-in tool. It rotates the three thumbnails across impressions, measures click-through rate plus watch-time signal, and tells you the winner.

Upload all three.

In Studio, open the Ep 01 video. In Thumbnail, click Test & Compare and add V1, V2, V3 as PNGs from the buttons above.

Let it run 7+ days.

YouTube needs enough impressions to call a winner with confidence. Don't peek at day three.

Read the verdict.

The winning thumbnail becomes the new default. The other two get archived to the variants library for round two.
