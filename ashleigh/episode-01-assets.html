Hey babe. Welcome to Big Girl Thoughts. For the very first episode, I sat down with my best friend Treasa McGinnis (you know her as Mama T) and asked her to tell the story. The whole story. Two weeks before her high school graduation, Treasa lost the woman who raised her in a car accident. By 17 she had legal guardianship of her six younger siblings to keep them out of foster care. She had a baby of her own. She walked through abuse, addiction, a season as a dealer and a dancer. Today she's four years into vocational ministry and she leads the Dream Team at our church. If you have ever felt like your story is too much, this one is for you. Mama T came on to tell the truth.
