The whole story.

The arc, in three beats.

Treasa walks me through the night her grandmother (the woman we all called Mama) was killed in a car accident on her senior prom night. She was 17, two weeks from graduation, and the oldest of seven. Overnight she took legal guardianship of her six siblings to keep them out of foster care.

What followed: an abusive relationship, her own baby born six days apart from her partner's outside child (raised together as twins), a season she calls jumping into the pit. Drugs, dancing, dealing. Then a red-haired stranger walked up to her in Greens Point Mall and invited her to church. The marriage that came next did not hold. The pit got deeper. And then a drive to Colleen, Texas, twice a week, started to change everything.

Four years into vocational ministry, Mama T leads the Dream Team at our church and lives by a line she coined herself. You'll hear it at 38:38.
