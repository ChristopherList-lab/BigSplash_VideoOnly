Operations, Culture & Team

With Katlin Sias

What gives a company the best chance to win in 10 years?

Setting vision and cultivating the unique abilities in your team.

  • Unique ability — the natural talent and passion they're wired to bring.
  • Where it shows up: social media management · Meta ads · editing · video sales · tracking · automations / AI · storytelling.
  • Focus on your unique ability — you'll be able to see it in others.

The Energy Audit

List the work that energizes you and that you do best.

Energy-giving
Client success & relationship building · Consulting · Idea ideation
Draining
Filming and editing

Talent alone makes you a creative. Operations, culture & team make you a wealthy one.

Culture

  • Culture is who moves the work — the values they live.
  • Every company has values on the wall; few live them.
  • Remember Enron. Their wall said Integrity, Communication, Respect, Excellence — then they became the biggest fraud in history.
Value reality check
  1. Based on who you last hired, promoted, or let go — what are your top three values?
  2. Yours: Loyalty · Excitement · Integrity
  • Culture is the caliber of the people beside you. If your people aren't okay, the business won't be okay.
  • Excellence — hold the work higher than anyone asks. Rather make it better than make excuses.
  • Curiosity / Innovation — question how it's always been done.
  • Honesty — direct and kind, even when the truth is hard.
  • Courage — say the hard thing even when it's not popular.
  • Teamwork — make others better and let them make you better.
  • Faithfulness — do what you said, show up when it's hard. See work as worship.

We are a championship team — not a comfortable one.

Culture in one line: surround good people with better people.

Operations

Operations is how the work moves. Simplicity scales; complexity does not.

  • It breaks two ways: 01 we do it to ourselves (shiny-object syndrome), or 02 growth outpaces us.

Session 03

4 Keys to Scaling

With Jake Sheldon

01 Get noticed — get leads

  • You're in the marketing business that does a great service. There's a lot of competition — but most businesses do the bare minimum.
  • Only 9% of businesses break the million-dollar revenue mark.

02 Effectively sell — handle objections

Deploy marketing

  • Look up David Ogilvy.
  • Post in local Facebook groups — find every local group in your area, post valuable content. Value first, don't prompt.
  • Build a Google Sheet of all groups in your demo. Pick 10 a day, 5 days a week.

Strategic partnerships

  • Partner with local businesses — you refer them customers, they refer you customers. But here's the kicker:
  • Instead of word of mouth, get them to send a text or email to their entire list.
  • Do the math: 3 partners × 1,000 customers = 3,000 people. 10% say yes → 300 new customers.

Gamify the giveaway

  • Stop giving credits for referrals. No credit.
  • Give a $50 Amazon gift card for every new customer, plus entry into a $500 Amazon giveaway every 6 months.
  • Why it works: people love winning stuff.

03 Delegation

You need SOPs for everything. The 55/5 rule: work 55 minutes like normal, then spend the last 5 writing down what you did. After two weeks, color-code each item — red (delegate, non-revenue), yellow (generates revenue but can be delegated), green (only the owner / high-leverage). Take one pass at a time.

Delegation — step by step
  1. Outline the task
  2. Define criteria — what done-right looks like
  3. Collect examples — screenshots, past work, videos
  4. Record yourself doing the work
  5. Transfer ownership of the task
  6. They watch the training and create their own checklist
  7. Review and answer their questions
  • Save thousands — delegate. VA at $5–10/hr overseas. Hire with a W-8 form.
  • Track leads · automate conversations · automate employee onboarding · automate social posts with AI.

04 Equity & commitment

  • You MUST have an operating agreement with an equity partner — vested equity.
  • The bigger the company, the more proof they'll have.
  • Only give the deal to 1-year commitments.
Session 04

How to Make High-Value Decisions

With Megan Rhodes · “She drew a tree.”

You can never outperform your identity.

We each have a tree we've built. Every tree has a root system — a whole bunch of roots. Identity builds deep roots. Owners love having their hands on their tree… but we need to redo the root system.

  • Branches — your skills and capabilities.
  • Fruit — your behaviors.

The tree, live: Son/Sun feeding it, skills & capabilities as branches, beliefs (“I am…”) and Jesus at the base, roots of identity feeding values. Bottom split — Achievement vs. Fulfillment, business vs. ministry — and the alignment order: Jesus · Spouse · Family · Everything else (work).

The 5 fruits (behavior patterns)

Which one is yours?
  1. Indispensable — “I'm only safe when I'm needed.” Attacks your body, relationships, capacity, faith, business. Hits your body and capacity hardest.
  2. Pleaser — “I'm only valuable when I'm useful to people.” You don't know what you want anymore — a state of confusion. You can't rest.
  3. Performer — “I'm only loved when I'm excellent.” You build a life where the stage is never empty. You always give the polished version of you.
  4. Prover — “I'm only worthy when I'm earning.” Can you turn the power off? We need to check in on each other when this pops up.
  5. Striver / Believer — “I'm only successful when I'm striving.” You're striving toward a God who already chose you.

There are two sides of success: achievement and fulfillment.

Is your business a business — or a ministry?

You're taking previous wounds and pouring them onto your business.

Draw a new tree

  • Invite the Lord into your identity. Surrender. Give up control.
  • If you're holding everything, you'll be slower.
  • Let your beliefs and values hold up your tree; let skills & capabilities water your life.
  • At first you may have to water it — but a proper tree knows how to grow.
  • Don't yell at yourself. Don't condemn yourself.
  • To get rid of these things, plant a new tree. Do the root work — it will change everything.
Note to self

Create capacity and space. You will be the ceiling in your business if you don't work on this.

(Side note: Landon should incorporate a lot of this for men.)

Beliefs

  • It goes from what happens to what am I making this mean.
  • Every belief has an “I am” statement. What happens after failure? What am I making it mean?
  • Beliefs and actions are two different things — you're allowed to change the response.
  • “This is just the kind of boss I am…” → you'll be bad. You are not stuck.

Grow up. Grow a new pair of beliefs. Grow a pair.

  • If you know you're dealing with a belief, pick the opposite.
  • If you gave yourself permission not to act like that — how would you act?

The 5-second rule — Megan quoting Lee Warren

  • Most impulses don't last more than 7 minutes. Emotions can fully pass if you let yourself feel them — resist them and they linger.
  • Separate thought from action. Can you challenge the first thought that pops up?
  • Some people turn inward, some outward: “I'm rejected.” “I'm not worth his time.” “I'm abandoned.”

Take 5 seconds and breathe through it. Is this true? Is it possible this isn't true?

  • Challenge the thought — practice not immediately taking action.
  • Thoughts turn into emotions. Pause before you act.

Alignment

Church kind of combines everything. The order that fills your bucket:

The order
  1. Jesus — fills your bucket to be a healthy person. Prayer relaxes your frontal lobe.
  2. Spouse — if work is affecting family, your wife can sense it. Done at 6pm means done at 6pm.
  3. Family — those who live under your roof.
  4. Everything else — work.
Non-negotiable

Minimum 30 min every morning. A dopamine detox — no phone, no email, no nothing — until you feel the presence of God. Plug back into the source.

  • Practical: get an analog alarm clock. Don't touch your phone for the first hour. Throw it somewhere else.
  • Most of us are addicted to our phones. Protect your ability to lead — be in the Word and disciplined.
  • When you hear the Lord, you won't be chasing shiny stuff. Anything can work — but hear what the Lord wants you to do. Certainty comes from Him.

Interdependence

What you do affects the other person. No “me” or “you” — it's a team. Your business affects every other area of your life.

  • If your life didn't look any different in 5 years, what would you think?
  • How do you want the business to feel in 5 years? Can you take your wife out? Can you go to the gym when you want?
  • Why does it matter to you? What will you do to protect it?
